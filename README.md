# Using Generative AI Tools - Boon or Bane
This repository contains all the files used for the study "Using Generative AI Tools - Boon or Bane".

1. Storage and Accessibility: Explain why it is important to store and organise your files eﬃciently. (2-3 sentences)
It is important to store and organise research files efficiently so that team members can easily find and access the data they need. A clear folder structure and consistent file naming can also reduce the risk of losing files, using incorrect versions, or duplicating data.

2. File Storage Methods: specify  which data types may require special access controls.Explain (2-3sentences)
I would store literature reviews, analysis scripts, drafts and reports in clearly labelled folders, while organising quantitative and qualitative data separately. Sensitive files, such as identifiable survey data, interview transcripts, consent forms, audio recordings and participant photos, require restricted access because they may contain personal or confidential information.

3. Why is it important to have a good naming convention?
A good naming convention helps researchers stay organised and quickly identify and locate files. It also makes it easier for team members to navigate shared research files and reduces the risk of files being misplaced or lost.
